# Tla – Privacy policy / سياسة الخصوصية

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

[العربية](#ar) · [English](#en)

<a id="en"></a>
## English

Tla is a Quran recitation app by Sindbad. It listens while you recite and follows you word by word. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

**In short: Tla has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your voice is processed on your phone and is never uploaded.**

### Microphone
Tla uses the microphone only while you recite (after you tap the microphone, or in the recording studio). The sound is turned into words by a speech model that runs **on your phone**. The audio is not sent to us or to anyone else.

### What stays on your phone
- Your recitation sessions, progress, streaks, the ayat you have to review and your exam results.
- Your settings (language, theme, goals, reminders, word colours).
- Downloaded recitations, tafsir books and tajweed fonts.

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

### Recordings you choose to send (recording studio)
In *Help Tla hear better* you can record short ayat to help improve the speech model. Before the first recording you agree to a release and give a name or initials. The recordings stay on your phone **unless you tap "Send recordings"**, which opens WhatsApp (or another app you choose) with the recordings and your name. If you send them, we use them only to train and test Tla's speech recognition. To have recordings you sent deleted, email us.

### Internet
Tla connects to the internet only to:
- check for a new version of the app (a small file on GitHub);
- download recitations, tafsir books or tajweed fonts when you ask for them (from everyayah.com, GitHub/jsDelivr and the Quran Foundation's servers).

These requests carry no personal information beyond what any internet request carries (such as your IP address, seen by those servers).

### Children
Tla is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

### Contact
Questions or requests: **elewa64@gmail.com**
